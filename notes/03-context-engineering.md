# 03 · Context Engineering

Context engineering decides what the model can see, in what form, and at what moment.

## Four layers

- Stable policy: durable rules and safety boundaries.
- Task state: current goal, constraints, and decisions.
- Retrieved evidence: minimal source material needed now.
- Recent trajectory: actions and observations required for continuity.

## Compression rule

Compress explanations aggressively, but preserve decisions, unresolved risks, identifiers, and evidence links verbatim when correctness depends on them.
